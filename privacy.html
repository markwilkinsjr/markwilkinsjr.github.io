Privacy Policy

How we access, use, store, and share Google user data.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who we are

Deal Making 10,000 Residual is an independent deal-making and revenue-share business operated by Mark Wilkins. We connect audience owners with product operators, structure revenue-share agreements, track conversions, and earn a percentage of the revenue those deals produce.

What this app does

This application is the internal operations tool for that business. It reads our own Google account data to run prospecting research, draft and send outreach, maintain prospect and deal records in Google Sheets, and report results. It is not a product offered to the public. No end user creates an account, and no data is sold to anyone.

We request only the specific permissions our operations genuinely require. The complete list:

How we use it

  • Research. Public websites and platforms are read to qualify prospects — audience size, offers, pricing, and contact routes. Public information only.
  • Outreach. Messages are drafted from that research and sent only after Mark approves them.
  • Record keeping. Every prospect, conversation, and deal outcome is logged so we never duplicate an approach or lose context.
  • Reporting. Activity summaries are generated so Mark can see deal pipeline health.

Who we share it with

Nobody, for our own account data. We do not sell, rent, or trade data. We do not share your Google account data with third parties for their own purposes.

We do share your information with the operators we introduce you to — but only with your specific permission, only the information needed to evaluate or execute a deal, and only after you agree to that introduction. You can withdraw permission at any time.

Retention

  • Prospect and deal records — kept while the relationship is active and for a reasonable period after, so we do not re-approach anyone.
  • Email — retained in the mailbox under normal Google account management. Messages are labeled and archived as part of routine organization.
  • Research artifacts — retained for reference and to avoid repeating work already done.

Data sharing and processors

We use Google Workspace to operate. Mail, files, and spreadsheets are stored on Google's infrastructure under our account's own security and access controls. No third-party advertising or data-broker networks receive our data.

Your rights and controls

  • Revoke access at any time. Google Account → Security → Third-party access → remove this app. Access stops immediately and you can delete the stored local credentials.
  • Delete data. Ask us to delete prospect records, notes, and files. Deleting the Google account or the connected app's data removes the remote copies.
  • Opt out of outreach. Reply with "stop" or ask at any time and you will be removed from all future contact.

Security

Credentials are stored locally on Mark's own machine and are never transmitted to any server we control. Access is limited to the account owner. Where a message is sent on your behalf, the content is approved by you first.

Children

This service is not directed to anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. Questions about privacy, your data, or this application can be sent to markrwilkinsjr@gmail.com.
