Five mechanics. One operating system.

Every deal fits one of five structures. The mechanic is chosen by what the audience actually needs and what the two operators can build together.

MECHANIC 01

Audience + Product Match

An audience owner who does not sell what their audience already wants. A product that fits them perfectly. We structure the revenue share between two operators who did not know each other.

MECHANIC 02

The Auction

Auction a free year of a program. The winner creates social proof. Every non-winning bidder becomes a warm lead who gets followed into trials and lower tiers.

MECHANIC 03

Cross-Audience Deals

Two non-competing audiences with overlapping customers. We introduce them, they cross-promote, and we take a cut of the combined revenue neither could reach alone.

MECHANIC 04

The Upsell Chain

The first sale is the entry point. The real money is every add-on the same customer buys next — tools, communities, services, coaching.

MECHANIC 05

Operator-to-Operator

Two operators who should be working together. Referral deals, revenue shares, white-label licensing, equity, launch partnerships.

The model

  • Residual, not one-time. Every structure is designed to keep paying. A split that stops when the ad spend stops is not a business.
  • The 30–50% split test. Every deal must clear it. If the operator could self-serve without us, it is not a deal worth brokering.
  • No inventory. No support burden. We do not build funnels for other people's products. We connect, structure, and broker.
  • Start small, scale big. Prove the model, then multiply it. Do not build what can be connected.
  • Audience ownership stays with the operator. We take a share of the revenue, never control of the list.
